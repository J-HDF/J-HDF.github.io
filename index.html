ACADEMIC PROFILE NAPLES, ITALY

Mathematical analysis
and its applications.

My research uses variational techniques to investigate mathematical problems arising in continuum mechanics and materials science.

I am particularly interested in magnetic skyrmions, liquid crystals and thin structures, as well as nonlinear partial differential equations and functional inequalities.

RESEARCH AREAS

Research

My work is at the interface of rigorous mathematical analysis and the modelling of physical phenomena, particularly in continuum mechanics and materials science.

SELECTED FOCUS

The mathematics of magnetic skyrmions

I investigate variational models for magnetic textures, chirality, thin films and the geometry of energy minimisers, with analytical and numerical approaches.

Related paper on ResearchGate ↗
01

Micromagnetics and magnetic textures

Equilibrium states, skyrmions, nonlocal magnetostatic interactions, the Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya interaction, and reduced models for thin magnetic structures.

02

Partial differential equations and inequalities

Regularity of nonlinear elliptic systems, harmonic maps, symmetry of minimisers, and functional inequalities in classical and variable-exponent spaces.

03

Nonlocal variational models and soft matter

Nonlocal-to-local limits, calculus of variations for liquid crystals, dimensional reduction, and analytical questions from continuum mechanics.
